      Privacy & how it works

      RoleMint processes your file on its Oracle server and sends extracted resume text and the job description to the AI provider you choose. We do not save uploads, drafts, job descriptions or keys in a database. Processing happens in memory and temporary files are cleaned up. Your browser may retain form data or downloads according to its settings. The selected provider has its own retention and data-use policies. Do not upload someone else's resume without permission.

      This is a small community deployment. If it is busy, please try again later. There is no guarantee of ATS scores, interview invitations or job offers.

      Based on the open-source Resume Matcher document parser. View RoleMint source.
