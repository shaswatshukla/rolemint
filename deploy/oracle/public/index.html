      Privacy & how it works

      RoleMint processes your file on its Oracle server. The prompt-only option makes no AI provider call; you decide where to paste the prompt and attach your resume. In-site AI tailoring sends extracted resume text and the job description to the provider you choose. We do not save uploads, drafts, job descriptions or keys in a database. Processing happens in memory and temporary files are cleaned up. Your browser may retain form data or downloads according to its settings. The selected provider has its own retention and data-use policies. Do not upload someone else's resume without permission.

      This is a small community deployment. If it is busy, please try again later. There is no guarantee of ATS scores, interview invitations or job offers.
